The system's only heading component — Geist Light at display sizes, Geist Medium at h1–h3.

```jsx
<Heading level={1} size="display1">Form before information</Heading>
<Heading level={2}>Selected work</Heading>
```

- Keep `level` semantic and override the look with `size` — a visually huge sub-heading is `level={2} size="display2"`.
- `tone="accent"` for the one periwinkle heading per page, at most.
